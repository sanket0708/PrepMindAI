# Groq API Setup Guide

## ✅ What is Groq?

Groq is a **fast, free AI API** that's perfect for your project! It offers:
- ⚡ **Very fast responses** (low latency)
- 🆓 **Generous free tier**: 30 requests/min, 14,400 requests/day, 6,000 tokens/min


Your app will now use Groq instead of Gemini.

## 📊 Free Tier Limits

- **30 requests per minute**
- **14,400 requests per day**
- **6,000 tokens per minute**

These limits are much more generous than Gemini's free tier!
